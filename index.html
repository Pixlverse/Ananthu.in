04 / The Observer

The chairs aren't the differentiator. The way I sit in them is.

I've been in these chairs — and I actually paid attention while I was there.
sit in one.
look from the other.

SITTING AS
LOOKING FROM

From the inside
  • You know the history.
  • You know why decisions were made.
  • You know what has already been tried.
  • You get used to things that might need questioning.
From the outside
  • I look around.
  • I ask questions.
  • I notice details.
  • I connect things that don't seem connected.
  • I look at it from different sides.

I'm not looking for what's wrong. I'm looking for what may be getting missed.
